Linqing Wang 王林青

Tencent Hunyuan · Multimodal Understanding & AIGC Generation

Hello! I work on large-scale multimodal foundation models at Tencent Hunyuan, focusing on omni-unified multimodal interaction, text-to-image and video generation, unified understanding–generation models, and post-training for diffusion systems.

I was previously involved in robotics and bionics research during graduate study at Beihang University (BUAA). Feel free to reach out if you would like to chat or discuss research ideas.
