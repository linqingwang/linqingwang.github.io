Tencent Hunyuan · Multimodal generation & computer vision

About

I work on large-scale multimodal foundation models and their applications at Tencent Hunyuan, focusing on text-to-image / video generation, unified understanding–generation models, and post-training for diffusion systems. I was previously involved in robotics and bionics research during graduate study at Beihang University (BUAA).
